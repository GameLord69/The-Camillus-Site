Saint Camillus de Lellis

Saint Camillus de Lellis was an Italian priest who dedicated his life to caring for the sick and suffering. His life changed from being a soldier to becoming a devoted servant of the sick.

Early Life of Saint Camillus

Saint Camillus de Lellis was born on May 25, 1550, in Bucchianico, Italy. His mother died when he was young, and he grew up with a difficult childhood. As a teenager, Camillus became interested in the military and eventually followed his father's career as a soldier.

Military Career of Saint Camillus

Camillus joined the Venetian army and later served with the Spanish army. He fought in wars against the Ottoman Turks. During his military years, he developed a gambling habit and experienced poverty and hardship. A serious wound on his leg eventually prevented him from continuing his military career.

Conversion of Saint Camillus

While receiving treatment for his leg at a hospital in Rome, Camillus began to change his life. He became deeply devoted to God and decided to dedicate himself to serving the sick and suffering. His conversion became a major turning point, leading him away from his former lifestyle and toward religious service.

Work of Saint Camillus

Camillus devoted himself to caring for sick people, especially those who were poor or neglected. In 1582, he established a group dedicated to serving the sick, which later became the Order of Clerks Regular, Ministers to the Sick, commonly known as the Camillians. He taught his followers to care for patients with compassion, seeing their service as a way of serving Christ.

Death and Canonization of Saint Camillus

Camillus died on July 14, 1614, in Rome at the age of 64. His dedication to the sick continued to inspire the Church after his death. He was beatified in 1742 and canonized by Pope Benedict XIV in 1746. Saint Camillus is recognized as the patron saint of the sick, hospitals, nurses, and healthcare workers.

Saint Camillus at a Glance

Saint Camillus de Lellis is the patron saint of the sick, hospitals, nurses, and healthcare workers. His feast day is celebrated on July 14.
